HOW TO APPLY FOR ACADEMIC CONSIDERATION AT UOW

To apply for Academic Consideration, follow these steps:
  1. Log in to SOLS.
  2. Select 'Current Session' from the left-hand navigation menu.
  3. Select 'Academic Consideration' from the drop-down list.
  4. Select 'Create a new application' and read the information and instructions.
  5. When prompted, provide information about:
    1. The subject and assessment tasks you are requesting consideration for (if you aren't sure of the information, you can check your subject outline).
    2. The nature of assistance (type of consideration) you are seeking (e.g. extension of time to submit a task).
    3. Your circumstances.
  6. When prompted, choose the category of supporting documentation that applies to you:
    1. Provide documentation now.
    2. Provide documentation within three (3) working days of the task due date - you will need to return to your application and add document/s no later than three (3) working days after the due date. The documentation must cover the period you are requesting consideration for.
    3. Supporting documentation not required – only choose this if you are eligible to self-certify your application.
  7. If you are required to provide supporting documentation, when prompted:
    1. Select the document type from the dropdown.
    2. Attach the document.
    3. Complete all mandatory fields.
    4. Read and agree to the terms.
    5. Click upload.
  8. If required, add the next subject and assessment task you are requesting consideration for, and follow steps 5-7 above.
  9. After you have selected all the subjects and assessment tasks you are requesting consideration for, read and accept the declaration.
  10. Submit your application OR save it as a draft to return to it later.

    11. Please note, if you have saved your application as a draft or elected to provide your supporting documentation within three (3) working days of the task due date, your application will not be reviewed until it has been submitted and you receive the confirmation.

    For help regarding Academic Consideration eligibility or the application process, please contact the AskUOW team.

SUPPORTING DOCUMENTATION

You will need to provide supporting documentation with your application (unless your application is eligible for self-certification), or save your application and add supporting documentation within 3 working days of the assessable item's due date.

Applications that are eligible for self-certification (no documentation required) are:

  • The first application for self-certification in a session (e.g. Autumn, Spring, Trimester);
  • The assessment task is a written task (e.g. report or essay);
  • The request is for an extension of no more than three (3) days;
  • It is an individual task (not group work);
  • The request is made at least one day before the assessment deadline; and
  • All other eligibility criteria are met.

APPLICATION OUTCOMES

Applications for Academic Consideration can only be approved where the circumstances and supporting documentation meet the eligibility criteria outlined in the Student Academic Consideration PolicyStudent Academic Consideration Policy.

Approved Academic Considerations can result in:

  1. An extension of time to submit an assessment task.
  2. Permission to undertake a deferred assessment task or in-session test.
  3. Permission to undertake a deferred end of session exam.
  4. Consideration of compulsory attendance or participation.

NEXT STEPS

Once you have submitted your application and any required supporting documentation, the outcome of your Academic Consideration application will be sent to your SOLSMail within five (5) working days following the submission of supporting documentation, if required. Further information may also be sent to your UOWMail.

Please note, Academic Consideration applications that require supporting documentation will not be assessed until documentation is provided.

If you have not received an outcome and it has been more than five (5) working days from the date documentation was submitted:

  • Check your SOLS and UOWMail to see if you have been contacted for any further information to clarify your request.
  • You can contact the AskUOW team for further advice.
While you are waiting for the outcome of your application, you should proceed to work on any assessments to the best of your ability based on your circumstances.

For information about what you can do if your Academic Consideration application isn't approved, see 'What can I do if my Academic Consideration application is not approved?'
